# CineForge AI — End-User & Instructor Manual

## 1. Quick Start Walkthrough

### 1.1 Account Registration
1. Navigate to `http://localhost:3000/register`.
2. Enter your Display Name, Email address, and a secure password (minimum 8 characters).
3. Click **Register Account**. The system automatically logs you in and directs you to the Creative Studio Dashboard.

### 1.2 Creating a Video Project
1. In the sidebar, select **Projects**.
2. Click **Create Project**.
3. Enter a Title (e.g., *"Sci-Fi Teaser"*) and optional synopsis.
4. Click **Save Project**.

### 1.3 Generating Your First Video (Text-to-Video)
1. Select **Create Studio** in the sidebar.
2. Select your active project in the top dropdown.
3. Select the **Text to Video** tab.
4. Type a scene idea into the prompt box (e.g., *"Astronaut discovering an ancient temple on Mars, volumetric sunset light"*).
5. Click **AI Enhance** to automatically expand the prompt with cinematic camera movement, lighting, and style cues.
6. Configure your desired Aspect Ratio (16:9, 9:16, 1:1), Duration (5s or 10s), and Resolution (720p or 1080p).
7. Review the **Estimated Cost** calculation.
8. Click **Generate Video**.

### 1.4 Generating an Image-to-Video Sequence
1. Select the **Image to Video** tab.
2. Click the upload zone to select a starting frame (`.png`, `.jpg`, `.webp`).
3. Describe the intended camera motion or character action in the prompt box.
4. Click **Generate Video**.

### 1.5 Previewing and Downloading Output
1. The right-hand **Output Monitor** tracks the job lifecycle (`SUBMITTED` -> `PROCESSING` -> `SUCCEEDED`).
2. Once complete, the interactive video player loads the rendered video.
3. Click **Download MP4** to save the video directly to your computer.
